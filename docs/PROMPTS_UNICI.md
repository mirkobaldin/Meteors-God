# Prompt unici per Codex: comete e props

Due prompt da incollare in Codex dentro la cartella `Meteors-God`. Ognuno genera **tutte** le immagini della sua
categoria in un colpo solo. Quando hai approvato le immagini, i modelli 3D li farà il Claude in Blender.

---

## Prompt 1: Comete (13 tavole)

```
Generate 13 separate images, one per meteor, and save each one as PNG with a fully transparent background in
art/concepts/meteors/<name>.png (1536x1024), using exactly these file names: pebble, ember, frost, spark, venom,
crystal, magma, aurora, solar, royal_comet, void, supernova, blue_comet. Overwrite old files with the same name.

Game: "Meteor Gods" (Roblox). Players are young gods who tame LIVING METEORS and hurl them at giant planets to
shatter them. The meteors are the heroes of the game: each one is a living rock with a face, and they must look
BADASS, powerful and dangerous, like a boss monster or a legendary weapon, NOT cute, NOT cuddly, NOT a mascot.

Each image is a design sheet for ONE meteor:
- big 3/4 hero view on the left;
- on the right: front view and side view, plus the same meteor with 3 expressions (angry, furious with open mouth
  roaring, dazed);
- the body is roughly a sphere (it must read as a ball from far away) but with a strong silhouette: jagged rock
  plates, sharp edges, cracks, horns or spikes, crystals or metal where the element asks for it;
- the face is SCULPTED into the rock, not a sticker: deep-set narrow glowing eyes under heavy rock brows that are
  always frowning, a wide mouth full of jagged teeth, NO blush, NO big round cute eyes;
- no flame trail, no motion blur, no VFX around it, no background: only the meteor itself.

Rarity must show in the shape: Common = simple rough rock with a few cracks; Uncommon = more defined, some element
growths; Rare = horns or big crystals; Epic = armored plates and a strong glow; Legendary = crown-like or royal
features, gold and light; Mythic = a unique, terrifying shape that looks like the final boss.

The 13 meteors (element / rarity / what makes it unique):
1. pebble: grey stone / Common / a rough cracked boulder with a scar over one eye, hard and stubborn.
2. ember: embers / Common / charcoal-black rock split by glowing orange cracks, smoldering.
3. frost: ice / Uncommon / blue-white rock covered in sharp ice shards, frozen breath, icy blue eyes.
4. spark: lightning / Uncommon / dark rock with copper veins and crackling yellow electric cracks, two metal
   lightning-rod horns.
5. venom: poison / Rare / dark green-purple rock with toxic glowing green cracks, two curved horns, dripping fangs.
6. crystal: gems / Rare / grey rock bursting with big sharp pink and violet crystals, crystal teeth.
7. magma: lava / Epic / black basalt armor plates over molten lava, two big curved horns, lava dripping from the
   mouth.
8. aurora: northern lights / Epic / dark blue rock with green-teal-violet glowing veins like an aurora, smooth
   polished plates.
9. solar: sun / Legendary / golden-orange molten rock with a spiked crown of solar flares, blinding white-gold eyes.
10. royal_comet: royal ice / Legendary / pale blue-white comet rock with a gold crown and gold armor bands, a
    noble and arrogant king.
11. void: black hole / Mythic / pitch-black rock with a single huge violet eye in the middle, purple cracks,
    reality bending around its edges, very creepy.
12. supernova: exploding star / Mythic / white-hot core breaking through red and gold armor plates, rays of light
    leaking from every crack, about to explode.
13. blue_comet: speed / Epic / streamlined blue rock shaped for speed, swept-back spikes like a helmet, cyan
    glowing eyes.

Art style: high-end stylized 3D game asset, like the best Roblox showcase games and modern action games: chunky
readable shapes, rich PBR materials (rough stone, glowing emissive cracks, translucent ice and crystal, polished
gold), dramatic but clean lighting, same scale and same camera for all 13 so they look like one family.
Moderate detail so it can be rebuilt in Blender. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermarks, no existing
characters (nothing that looks like Calcifer or other known characters). Fully transparent background, PNG.
```

## Prompt 2: Props (16 oggetti)

```
Generate 16 separate images, one per prop, and save each one as PNG with a fully transparent background in
art/concepts/props/<name>.png (1024x1024), using exactly these file names. Overwrite old files with the same name.

Game: "Meteor Gods" (Roblox). Players are young gods living on floating islands at the edge of the universe, in
modern luxury villas with big glass walls. The islands have green lawns, light rock cliffs and bridges of glowing
cosmic glass, under a purple nebula sky. These props decorate the villa gardens, the central island and the
bridges: together they must look like a luxury resort in space.

Each image shows ONE prop: a big 3/4 view, plus a small front view and top view.

The 16 props:
1. star_lamp: a slim garden lamp post in brushed gold with a frosted glass orb holding a tiny star.
2. meteor_sculpture: a rough glowing meteor fragment on a white marble plinth with a gold ring around it.
3. glass_gazebo: a small round gazebo with a thin white roof disk, glass panels and two wooden benches inside.
4. reflecting_pool: a rectangular shallow pool in travertine with stepping stones and soft underwater light.
5. armillary_sphere: a large gold armillary sphere on a marble base with a small glowing planet in the middle.
6. crystal_planter: a white stone planter with luminous cosmic plants and small cyan crystals.
7. lounge_set: two modern wooden sun loungers with white cushions and a small glass side table.
8. fire_pit: a round travertine fire pit with violet-blue cosmic flames and a curved wooden bench.
9. telescope_deck: a small wooden deck with a big elegant brass and white telescope pointing at the sky.
10. floating_stones: a path of five flat white stepping stones floating at different heights with a soft glow
    under them.
11. cosmic_tree: a sculptural tree with a pale trunk and a canopy of soft glowing violet leaves, in a round stone bed.
12. pergola: a modern wooden pergola with thin gold beams and hanging warm lights over an outdoor dining table.
13. name_pillar: an elegant entrance pillar of pale marble with a gold frame for a name plate and a light on top.
14. water_fountain: a minimalist fountain made of three offset marble slabs with water falling between them.
15. god_statue: a modern white marble statue of a young god throwing a meteor, smooth stylized forms, gold base.
16. bridge_lamps: a pair of tall slim light pylons in glass and gold to place along the cosmic glass bridges.

Art style: elegant "celestial modern" design, the same world as the gods' villas: white travertine and pale
marble, warm light wood, brushed gold, clear and frosted glass, warm lights and small soft cyan/violet starlight
accents. Bright, premium and clean, stylized for a Roblox game with clear readable shapes and moderate detail, easy
to rebuild in Blender with PBR materials. Same lighting and same camera angle for all 16 so they look like one set.
NO obsidian, NO black stone, NO spikes, NO gothic, NO dark fantasy, NOT cartoon-cute. Soft daylight. No text, no
letters, no logos, no watermarks, no existing brands. Isolated subject only, NO background, NO scenery, NO sky,
NO floor shadow plane: fully transparent background, PNG.
```
