# Comete: roster v2 (5 rarità × 5 + 2 Extraterrestri)

Rifatte da zero. Regole: corpo **compatto e tondeggiante** (sono proiettili da lanciare), **nessuna scia**, sempre
**due occhi**, e **ogni cometa ha una faccia diversa** (forma degli occhi, bocca, denti, carattere). Le descrizioni
sono in inglese perché finiscono nei prompt; sono le stesse di `src/shared/Config/Meteors.lua` (`look`, `expression`).
I vecchi nomi nei salvataggi vengono convertiti in automatico (`LEGACY_IDS` in `DataService`).

## ⚪ Comune
- **Grumble**: a lumpy grey moon-rock ball pocked with little craters, one bigger crater on its forehead like a bump. *Faccia:* heavy half-closed grumpy eyes with tiny pupils; a flat wide frown with one chipped front tooth sticking up; a grumpy old rock.
- **Cinder**: a coal-black ball speckled with glowing orange embers, a short curl of solid smoke on top like a cowlick. *Faccia:* huge round excited eyes glowing orange; a wide open laughing mouth with one single tooth; a hyperactive kid.
- **Flurry**: a packed snowball with a messy hairdo of icicle spikes on top, frosty blue shadows. *Faccia:* squinting mischievous eyes; a cheeky grin with two big buck teeth of ice; a prankster.
- **Zap**: a round ball of bright yellow static fur standing on end, two lightning-bolt shaped ear spikes. *Faccia:* wide shocked eyes with tiny dot pupils; a zigzag mouth; always startled.
- **Sprout**: a mossy green rock ball with a leafy sprout and tiny glowing mushrooms on its head. *Faccia:* eyes closed in happy upside-down U curves; a small content smile and moss-pink cheeks; gentle and calm.

## 🔵 Rara
- **Magmaw**: a cracked black basalt shell over a molten core, two stubby horns, lava drooling from the jaw. *Faccia:* angry slanted eyes glowing yellow; a huge underbite jaw with two tusks pointing up; a bully.
- **Gloop**: a bubbling toxic-green slime ball with a crusty purple rock crust grown over its top, bubbles rising inside. *Faccia:* mismatched crazy eyes, one big and one small; a wide wobbly grin with the tongue lolling out; completely unhinged.
- **Geode**: a plain brown rock ball split open horizontally: the split is its mouth, lined with purple amethyst crystals that glow inside. *Faccia:* two small faceted amethyst eyes, narrowed and cunning; the mouth is the geode crack with crystal teeth; sly.
- **Puffer**: a deep-space pufferfish puffed into a ball, covered in short spines, teal with glowing bioluminescent spots, small fins. *Faccia:* big round watery fish eyes; a tiny puckered O-shaped mouth; nervous.
- **Scarab**: a space beetle rolled up into an armored ball: glossy metallic green-blue shell plates, two short antennae. *Faccia:* two big glossy black bug eyes with a white highlight; small clicking mandibles; busy and fast.

## 🟣 Epica
- **Aurowl**: a round night-sky owl: feathers like dark blue crystal plates, green-violet northern lights flowing across its chest, two crystal ear tufts. *Faccia:* huge calm round owl eyes with green-violet aurora irises; a small closed hooked beak; wise.
- **Bastion**: an armored knight ball of black volcanic glass with sharp faceted plates and a visor ridge across the face. *Faccia:* two eyes glowing red through the narrow visor slit; the mouth hidden behind a grille of glass plates; stoic and silent.
- **Stormram**: a thundercloud ram: a body of dark sculpted storm-cloud wool with lightning glowing inside, two big curled ram horns of rock. *Faccia:* fierce narrowed eyes glowing white-blue; gritted teeth with sparks between them; aggressive.
- **Prism**: a faceted clear gem ball that splits light into rainbows. *Faccia:* diamond-shaped eyes; a smug one-sided smirk; arrogant.
- **Specter**: a translucent violet ghost orb with a soft wavy bottom edge (no wisps) and a dark rock skull-core visible inside. *Faccia:* hollow round eye sockets with tiny white pinpoint pupils; a wide stitched creepy grin; unsettling.

## 🟡 Leggendaria
- **Solaris**: a miniature sun of boiling plasma with a crown of solar-flare loops arching over the top (attached loops, nothing trailing). *Faccia:* confident narrowed eyes, white-hot; a proud closed-mouth smile; a king.
- **Leviathan**: a round deep-space whale creature, navy blue with glowing constellation spots, small fins, ancient and majestic. *Faccia:* small wise ancient eyes; a huge wide gentle mouth with baleen plates; serene.
- **Phoenix**: a round fire bird with its wings folded tight around its body like a cloak and a crest of flame feathers (nothing trailing behind). *Faccia:* sharp golden hawk eyes; a hooked beak open in a battle cry; proud and fierce.
- **Frostfang**: a compact ice dragon head shaped like a ball: curled ice horns and a ridge of frost spikes along the back. *Faccia:* icy blue reptile eyes with slit pupils; a fanged jaw with cold mist; predatory.
- **Midas**: a solid polished gold ball studded with cut gems. *Faccia:* round coin-like eyes with ruby pupils; a greedy grin full of gold teeth; greedy.

## 🔴 Mitica
- **Singularity**: a pitch-black sphere with a glowing violet accretion ring orbiting around its middle like a planet ring, light bending at its edges. *Faccia:* two glowing violet ring eyes with black centers; a mouth that is a swirling vortex; ominous.
- **Supernova**: a star bursting through cracked dark armor plates, white-gold light blasting out of every crack. *Faccia:* furious blazing eyes; a roaring wide-open mouth pouring light; pure rage.
- **Galaxy**: a sphere of dark glass with a whole spiral galaxy swirling inside, tiny planets orbiting close around it. *Faccia:* eyes with star-shaped pupils; a serene knowing smile; all-knowing.
- **Chrono**: a bronze clockwork sphere with two thin crossed rings orbiting it and gears visible through glass panels. *Faccia:* eyes that are two little clock dials with moving hands; a small precise smile with square cog teeth; calculating.
- **Eclipse**: a sphere split down the middle: one half a blazing golden sun, the other half a cold silver moon. *Faccia:* the face is split too: one eye golden fire, the other pale silver; the mouth half grin, half frown; two-faced.

## 🟢 Extraterrestre (solo dal cielo, niente fusione)
- **Visitor**: a smooth silver biomechanical orb with glowing green seams and a thin saucer ring around its middle. *Faccia:* huge black almond-shaped alien eyes; a tiny thin slit mouth, no nose; eerily calm.
- **Xenobloom**: an alien carnivorous seed-pod: dark purple chitin petals clamped shut around a glowing green core. *Faccia:* two glowing green slit eyes between the petals; the petals part like a mouth showing rows of glowing green needle teeth; predatory.

## Armi
Quando hai trovato **tutte le comete di una rarità** (l'Index se lo ricorda) sblocchi l'arma di quella rarità e
**scegli tu** quale delle sue comete ne diventa la testa: nell'Index clicchi una cometa di quella rarità (⚔ = scelta
attuale). Comune → Club, Rara → Hammer, Epica → Lance, Leggendaria → Scythe, Mitica → Mace, Extraterrestre → Ray Lance
(es. "Frostfang Scythe"). Colpisci un giocatore, lo sbalzi e gli fai cadere una cometa raccolta in questa pioggia.

Descrizioni per le immagini: `art/concepts/comets/comets.json`. Prompt: `docs/PROMPT_CODEX_COMETE.md`.
