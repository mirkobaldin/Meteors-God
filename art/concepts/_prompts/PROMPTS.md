# Prompt dei concept di Meteor Gods

Ogni `# IMAGE` è un'immagine: `run.py` la manda a Codex (generazione immagini integrata) con i blocchi di testo
indicati in `blocks:` e le immagini di riferimento in `refs:` (percorsi relativi ad `art/`, usate solo se esistono).
Per rifare un'immagine basta cancellarla e rilanciare `run.py`.

# BLOCK STYLE
ART STYLE (identical for every image of this game): polished modern Roblox toon style, as clean and readable as the
best-made Roblox simulator games of 2025-2026 (quality bar only: do not copy any existing game, character, logo or
brand). Chunky, soft, rounded, slightly oversized shapes with smooth bevels; clean readable contours with a subtle dark
outline; simple cel shading with soft gradients and glossy highlights; glowing neon accents. Game palette: white marble
and polished gold, neon purple/magenta and neon cyan, set against a deep blue-violet starry space sky with soft nebula
clouds. Saturated, clean colors. No photorealism, no grime, no noisy micro-detail, no painterly blur: it must look like
a crisp, game-ready stylized 3D render.

# BLOCK CITADEL
MAP FACTS (must be respected exactly): the Citadel of the Gods is ONE round floating platform (radius about 120 studs;
a player avatar is about 5 studs tall, so the platform is about 48 player-heights across) hanging in empty space at the
edge of the universe; below it there is only the starry void. The spawn hub is in the exact center. EXACTLY 8 player
house plots, evenly spaced in a ring (each plot is a round pad about 28 studs wide carrying a small shrine). EXACTLY 4
Star Bridges: rainbow light bridges (Bifrost-like ribbons of solid rainbow energy, about 12 studs wide and about 220
studs long, almost twice the platform radius) that ALL leave from the SAME side of the platform, the side facing the
planet, and stop abruptly in the void. At the end of each bridge there is a round floating LAUNCH PAD with a glowing
ring and an upward arrow rune. Far beyond the bridges (about 760 studs from the center) a GIANT planet hangs in space:
the first target, a rocky brown-grey planet with glowing orange cracks, filling a large part of the sky. Nothing else
around the citadel: no other islands, no ground, no ocean.

# BLOCK TOPVIEW
VIEW: top-down orthographic game map of the whole citadel, fully rendered in color (not a sketch, not a blueprint),
with the 4 bridges and their launch pads entirely visible and the planet partly visible at the edge in the bridge
direction. Small clean labels: SPAWN at the center, numbers 1 to 8 on the house plots, LAUNCH on each pad, a small
scale bar labelled "50 studs". Landscape 3:2 (1536x1024).

# BLOCK PLAYERVIEW
VIEW: third-person gameplay view from a player standing near the spawn (camera at about 1.5x avatar height, slightly
behind a small generic blocky Roblox-style avatar with a soft white aura; no recognizable character), looking toward
the 4 Star Bridges and the giant planet beyond them; a few house plots visible on the sides to show the scale.
The attached top view is the EXACT layout to follow: same shapes, same materials, same colors, same bridge placement.
Landscape 3:2 (1536x1024).

# BLOCK METEOR
SUBJECT: character design sheet of a LIVING METEOR, the collectible pet of the game. Rules shared by every meteor:
the body is a round, chunky ROCK ball (solid stone, clearly a rock: never a flame, never a blob of fire); a simple cute
face on the front of the rock: two big round glossy eyes with white highlights, a small expressive mouth, rosy blush
cheeks; FLAMES grow only from the top and the back of the rock like a hairstyle or mane, swept backwards, in the
element's colors. It must stay readable at a tiny size (big shapes, strong silhouette, few colors). Original design: it
must NOT look like Calcifer from Howl's Moving Castle or any other existing character (no body made of fire, no
candle-flame silhouette, no wide mouth full of jagged teeth). Rarity shows through the SHAPE: Common = simple lumpy
pebble; Uncommon = round rock with one element feature; Rare = sculpted rock with a strong element feature; Epic =
dramatic shape with big element parts; Legendary = regal shape with a crown; Mythic = a unique spectacular form unlike
any other meteor.
SHEET LAYOUT (landscape 3:2, 1536x1024, clean dark blue-violet starry background, same size and same 3/4 front angle
for every pose, every pose labelled in clean capitals): title at the top left with a rarity-colored badge (as given
below). TOP ROW: five expressions of the SAME meteor: SLEEPY (heavy half-closed eyes, a tiny "z", small calm flames),
HAPPY (big shining eyes, open smile, blushing cheeks, lively flames), ANGRY (slanted brows, frown, bigger flames),
FURIOUS (sharp V brows, shouting mouth, flames blazing tall with a glowing aura: it is being charged for launch),
DIZZY (spiral eyes, wobbly mouth, little stars circling, drooping flames). BOTTOM ROW: GOLDEN (the body turned into
polished gold, golden flames, sparkles), RAINBOW (iridescent rainbow-banded body, rainbow flames), ECLIPSED (black-violet
body with a glowing white-purple eclipse corona around its rim, dark violet flames, glowing eyes), IN FLIGHT (the happy
meteor flying with a long flame trail), and three tiny icon-size copies of the happy face to prove readability.

# BLOCK HOUSE
SUBJECT: concept sheet of the player's SANCTUARY (house) in Meteor Gods. Each player owns one on a round plot of the
citadel and it grows with the player's divine evolution, in 6 levels. HARD CONSTRAINTS: the whole sanctuary fits inside
a circle of radius 14 studs (a player is 5 studs tall, so the footprint is under 6 player-heights across; height is
free). Every sanctuary has EXACTLY 4 pedestals where the player's meteors float (3 standard pedestals + 1 "extra altar"
pedestal marked with a small glowing lock glyph), and one sign at the front showing the owner's name: "PLAYERNAME".
The front faces the viewer (the center of the citadel). LAYOUT (landscape 3:2, 1536x1024, dark starry background with a
soft floor glow): a big hero 3/4 view of the sanctuary with a small generic blocky avatar standing next to it for scale
and cute living meteors (round rocks with simple faces and flame hair) floating above the pedestals; a small top-down
footprint inset in a corner showing the dashed 14-stud radius circle, the 4 pedestals and the sign. Title at the top:
as given below.
